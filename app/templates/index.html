Questions

Why does a figure say Stale or Unavailable?

Figures are stored after each successful refresh from OpenDOSM. A figure is Stale when the last refresh failed or the provider's next scheduled update has passed, and Unavailable when nothing has been stored yet. In both cases the page shows the last good value and says why it is not current.

Do I need an account?

No. Public statistics, calculators, and learning pages are open to everyone. An account is only needed to save personal spending records and set inflation alerts.
